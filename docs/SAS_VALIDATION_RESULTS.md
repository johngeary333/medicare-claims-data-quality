# SAS Validation Results — CMS DE-SynPUF Sample 1

**Execution date:** October 26, 2026  
**Environment:** SAS Studio Online  
**Data source:** CMS DE-SynPUF Sample 1 (synthetic Medicare beneficiary and inpatient claims records)  
**Scripts executed:** [`00_import_and_verify.sas`](../sas/00_import_and_verify.sas), [`01_clean_and_quality_checks.sas`](../sas/01_clean_and_quality_checks.sas), and [`02_summary_and_export.sas`](../sas/02_summary_and_export.sas)

## Purpose and verification status

The project owner executed the three SAS programs in SAS Studio Online and compared the resulting summary tables with the previously generated Python/SQLite report. The **reported SAS counts, quality flags, age-group summaries, and month-by-month summaries agree with the checked-in Python/SQLite results** at the displayed precision. These tables were transcribed from the SAS output shared by the project owner. This is cross-tool agreement for a synthetic dataset, not a claim about real Medicare utilization.

## 6. Independent validation and limitations

- The SAS output matches the existing Python/SQLite report for the **key overall metrics, data-quality flags, age-group table, and monthly table at their displayed precision**.
- This comparison does not prove that every line of each script or every unlisted metric is identical. Values in the report are still generated by the Python/SQLite pipeline.
- Means for payments exclude missing and negative payment values; utilization-day means exclude missing and negative values.
- The analysis uses an **admission-year filter for 2008**, linked to the 2008 beneficiary snapshot, not the full three-year file as an analytic cohort.
- The CMS DE-SynPUF data are **synthetic** and are not suitable for population-level conclusions about actual Medicare utilization, costs, disease frequency, or healthcare-associated infections.
- Diagnosis codes are ICD-9-era synthetic codes, not ICD-10 or a validated HAI classification.
